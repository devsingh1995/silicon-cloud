Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before you plan your video analytics project.

Does the platform analyse every video frame?

Not necessarily. The processing system can select frames based on model requirements and available resources. The chosen rate must still be sufficient for reliable counting or event detection.

Can the platform show actual real-time visitor counts?

Near-real-time counts can be a design objective, but update frequency depends on camera connectivity, processing settings and tested implementation.

Are alerts proof of security incidents?

No. Alerts indicate configured conditions that require staff review. They are not proof of wrongdoing or intent.

Can reporting cover multiple stores?

Central reporting is part of the proposed platform design. Consistent installation and reporting rules are necessary for useful comparisons.

How is AI accuracy assessed?

The system should use task-specific validation: precision and recall for detection tasks, counting error for entry counts and false-alert reviews for events.

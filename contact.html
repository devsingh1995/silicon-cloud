WHO WE ARE

Practical AI for the physical retail world.

Retail cameras capture continuous visual information, yet teams often rely on manual observation to understand customer traffic, queue activity and selected operational events.

SiliconCloud focuses on computer vision, cloud GPU processing and simple reporting workflows that can help retailers turn those camera observations into information people can use.

Our approach starts with a defined use case, suitable camera coverage and validation in the intended environment. We do not treat AI alerts as conclusive evidence or present untested performance figures as production results.

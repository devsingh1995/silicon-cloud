Privacy policy.

How we handle information submitted through the SiliconCloud website.

Last updated: 26 October 2026.

This policy describes the intended handling of information submitted through siliconcloud.space. SiliconCloud is • Born in India. Built for All.. Contact us at contact@siliconcloud.space.

Information you provide

When you contact us, you may provide your name, business email address, company name, phone number, estimated store and camera counts, selected monitoring requirements and message. Please avoid sending sensitive CCTV footage or personal information about other people through the public enquiry form.

How we use enquiry details

We use the information you submit to review your enquiry, communicate with you and discuss a potential retail analytics project. We do not use the public enquiry form to collect payment details.

Service providers

The website is designed for hosting on Cloudflare Pages. Enquiry emails are sent through a separately configured email delivery provider, if enabled. Those providers may process limited technical or enquiry information to provide hosting or message delivery services. Review the relevant provider terms before production deployment.

Retention and security

Enquiry data should be retained only as long as necessary for legitimate communication and applicable legal obligations. Access should be limited to authorised personnel. No online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Site owners must configure hosting access, email delivery and retention before launch.

Cookies and website analytics

This template does not include advertising pixels or a third-party web analytics script. Your hosting provider may process routine technical logs. If you later enable analytics or optional cookies, update this policy and implement any notices or choices required by applicable law.

CCTV video processed by the product

This marketing website does not collect live CCTV streams. For any video analytics product deployment, processing purposes, customer roles, access controls, geographic transfer, retention and applicable legal obligations should be documented separately in relevant customer agreements and notices.

Your questions and requests

For privacy enquiries, access or correction requests, or questions about information you have provided through this website, contact contact@siliconcloud.space. We will review requests according to applicable law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this notice as the website, product capabilities and hosting arrangements develop. The revision date above indicates when it was last updated.

Publication note: This is a starting policy for the supplied website implementation and should be reviewed by a qualified professional against actual operations before launch.
